# Article 01 — What Is Schizophrenia?

## Purpose

Establish the conceptual foundation of the 50-article Schizophrenia Research Journey.

## Core Question

What exactly is schizophrenia, and why has it been difficult to map the diagnosis onto a single biological mechanism?

## Central Thesis

Schizophrenia is a clinically recognizable syndrome whose biological mechanisms remain incompletely understood. Current evidence supports substantial clinical and biological heterogeneity and does not identify a single necessary and sufficient cause.

## Evidence Discipline

Throughout this project, clearly distinguish:

- Established scientific findings
- Strongly supported interpretations
- Plausible interpretations
- Open scientific questions
- Original proposed hypotheses

The Precision Instability Framework (PIF) is an original proposed research hypothesis and must not be presented as an established theory of schizophrenia.
