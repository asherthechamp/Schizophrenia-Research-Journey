# Schizophrenia and Biomarkers

## Current Status

Many potential biological biomarkers have been investigated, including genetic, molecular, neuroimaging, and electrophysiological measures.

## Key Finding

No single biomarker currently provides a definitive routine clinical diagnosis of schizophrenia.

## Important Distinctions

Biological group difference ≠ diagnostic biomarker

Prediction ≠ explanation

Correlation ≠ causation

Group classification ≠ individual mechanism

## Research Implication

Future biomarker research should examine biological subgroups, symptom dimensions, mechanisms, and individual-level prediction.
