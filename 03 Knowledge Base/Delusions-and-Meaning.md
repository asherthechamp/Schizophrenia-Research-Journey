# Delusions and Meaning

## Definition

Delusions are persistent beliefs that are not adequately explained by the person's cultural or social context and that are held with varying degrees of conviction.

## Research Perspective

A major research question concerns how changes in significance, evidence evaluation, uncertainty, inference, and meaning contribute to delusion formation and maintenance.

## Related Concepts

- Anomalous salience
- Prediction
- Belief updating
- Uncertainty
- Inference
- Precision

## Evidence Status

Delusions are established clinical phenomena.

Specific computational and phenomenological explanations remain under investigation.
