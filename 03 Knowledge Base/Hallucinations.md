# Hallucinations

## Definition

Hallucinations are perceptual experiences occurring without a corresponding external stimulus of the relevant kind.

## Schizophrenia Context

Auditory verbal hallucinations are common psychotic phenomena but are highly heterogeneous.

Relevant dimensions include:

- Agency
- Localization
- Emotional tone
- Frequency
- Controllability
- Meaning
- Relationship to thought

## Evidence Status

Hallucinations are well-established clinical phenomena.

Their underlying mechanisms remain an active research area.

## Open Questions

How do internally generated representations acquire the phenomenological qualities of externally generated perceptions?
