# Minimal Self

## Definition

The minimal self refers to the most basic first-person character of experience: the implicit sense that experience is occurring for or from a particular subject.

## Related Concepts

- Ipseity
- Self-presence
- Agency
- Ownership
- Phenomenology

## Evidence Status

An important philosophical and phenomenological framework used in schizophrenia research.

It should not be treated as a validated biological mechanism.

## Open Questions

How can the minimal self be operationalized and connected to measurable neural and computational processes?
