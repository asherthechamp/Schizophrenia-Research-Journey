# Negative Symptoms

## Definition

Negative symptoms involve reductions or diminished expression of normally occurring psychological or behavioral functions.

Common domains include:

- Avolition
- Anhedonia
- Asociality
- Alogia
- Diminished emotional expression

## Important Distinction

Negative symptoms are multidimensional.

Primary negative symptoms should be distinguished from secondary phenomena caused or influenced by other factors such as depression, medication effects, anxiety, psychosis, or social circumstances.

## Evidence Status

Negative symptoms are an established component of schizophrenia-spectrum psychopathology.

Their mechanisms are heterogeneous and incompletely understood.
