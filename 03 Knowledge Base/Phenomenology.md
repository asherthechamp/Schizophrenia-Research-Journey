# Phenomenology

## Definition

Phenomenology is the systematic description and investigation of the structure and content of subjective experience.

## Why It Matters

Schizophrenia involves phenomena that cannot be completely described by observable behavior alone.

Phenomenology asks what experiences such as hallucinations, delusions, altered agency, and avolition are like from the first-person perspective.

## Related Concepts

- Self-disorder
- Minimal self
- Agency
- Ownership
- Hallucination
- Delusion
- Anomalous salience
- Negative symptoms

## Evidence Status

Established as a legitimate methodological and clinical research tradition.

It is not itself a complete biological theory of schizophrenia.

## Open Questions

How can phenomenological descriptions be connected to cognitive, computational, and neural mechanisms?
