# Psychiatric Diagnosis

## Core Concept

Psychiatric diagnoses are clinical classification constructs used to identify patterns of symptoms, behavior, functioning, and course.

## Key Distinction

A diagnostic category does not necessarily correspond to a single biological mechanism.

## Application to Schizophrenia

Schizophrenia is clinically diagnosable even though no single biological biomarker currently defines the disorder.

## Research Implication

Research should distinguish:

Clinical phenotype
→ Psychological processes
→ Biological mechanisms
→ Computational mechanisms
