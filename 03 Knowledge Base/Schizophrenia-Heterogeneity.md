# Schizophrenia Heterogeneity

## Definition

Heterogeneity refers to substantial variation among people who receive the same schizophrenia diagnosis.

## Dimensions of Variation

- Positive symptoms
- Negative symptoms
- Cognitive impairment
- Disorganization
- Mood symptoms
- Functional outcomes
- Illness trajectory
- Genetic risk
- Environmental risk
- Treatment response

## Scientific Importance

Heterogeneity creates challenges for identifying universal biomarkers and mechanisms.

## Caution

Heterogeneity does not by itself prove that schizophrenia consists of multiple distinct diseases.

It supports continued investigation of multiple mechanisms and dimensions.
