# Schizophrenia

## Definition

Schizophrenia is a clinically defined psychiatric disorder characterized by disturbances that can include psychotic symptoms, disorganization, negative symptoms, and cognitive difficulties.

## Important Distinction

Schizophrenia is not synonymous with psychosis.

Psychotic phenomena can occur in multiple psychiatric and medical conditions.

## Scientific Status

Schizophrenia is a well-established clinical diagnosis, but its underlying biological mechanisms remain incompletely understood.

## Heterogeneity

Substantial variation exists among people diagnosed with schizophrenia in symptoms, course, cognition, functioning, biology, and treatment response.

## Research Question

A major contemporary question is whether schizophrenia represents one disease process or a heterogeneous syndrome produced by multiple interacting mechanisms.
