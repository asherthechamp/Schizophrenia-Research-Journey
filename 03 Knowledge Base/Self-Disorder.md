# Self-Disorder

## Definition

Self-disorder refers to proposed disturbances in basic or pre-reflective self-experience investigated particularly in schizophrenia-spectrum phenomenology.

## Related Concepts

- Ipseity
- Minimal self
- Self-presence
- Hyperreflexivity
- Agency
- Ownership
- First-person perspective

## Evidence Status

Important phenomenological research construct.

Not an established complete explanation of schizophrenia.

## Open Questions

- Specificity to schizophrenia-spectrum conditions
- Relationship to psychosis
- Relationship to neural mechanisms
- Whether self-disturbance is primary or secondary
