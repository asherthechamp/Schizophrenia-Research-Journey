## Scientific Governance

The Precision Instability Framework (PIF) is an original proposed research hypothesis developed within this project.

PIF is not an established theory of schizophrenia and must not be presented as established scientific fact.

Established findings, interpretations, open questions, and proposed hypotheses must remain clearly separated throughout the research journey.
