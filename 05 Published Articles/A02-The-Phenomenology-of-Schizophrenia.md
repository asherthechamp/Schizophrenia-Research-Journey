## Published Title

The Phenomenology of Schizophrenia

## Subtitle

What happens when the organization of experience begins to change?

## Core Question

> What is it actually like to experience schizophrenia?

## Central Theme

Article 2 moves from the diagnostic description of schizophrenia toward
the lived experience of schizophrenia.

It introduces phenomenology as a complementary level of investigation
and explores:

- hallucinations
- delusions and salience
- thought organization
- thought ownership
- agency
- minimal self
- ipseity
- negative symptoms
- anhedonia
- computational psychiatry
- the relationship between experience and biology

## Research Bridge

Experience
→ cognition
→ computation
→ neural circuits
→ biology

The reverse direction is also considered:

Biology
→ neural dynamics
→ computation
→ cognition
→ experience

This is a research framework, not an established causal hierarchy.

## PIF Status

The Precision Instability Framework (PIF) is explicitly presented as an
original proposed research hypothesis.

PIF is NOT presented as an established theory of schizophrenia.
