## Core Question

What happens when the brain's predictions do not match incoming
evidence?

---

## Core Concept

Prediction error describes a discrepancy between an expectation and an
observation.

Such discrepancies can provide information for learning, inference, and
model updating.

---

## Scientific Position

Prediction error is an established computational concept used across
multiple research frameworks.

However, sensory prediction error, reward prediction error, surprise,
salience, and dopamine signaling should not be treated as identical
concepts.

Predictive processing provides one framework for investigating
psychosis, but it is not an established complete explanation of
schizophrenia.
