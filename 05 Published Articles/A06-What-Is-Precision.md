## Research position

Precision is an established statistical concept and an important
component of several computational models of perception and inference.

Predictive-processing theories propose relationships between precision,
prediction error, attention, and inference. Their application to
psychosis remains an active area of research.

The Precision Instability Framework (PIF) presented in this series is a
proposed research hypothesis, not an established scientific theory.
