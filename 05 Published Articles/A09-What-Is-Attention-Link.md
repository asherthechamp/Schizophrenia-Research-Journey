## Article Summary

Article 9 explores attention as the brain's mechanism for prioritizing information in an environment containing far more sensory and cognitive information than can be processed equally.

The article examines:

- Why attention is necessary in an information-rich world
- Selective attention
- Goal-directed and top-down attention
- Stimulus-driven attention
- The relationship between attention and salience
- Attention and consciousness
- Attention as a distributed brain function
- Attention and predictive processing
- Precision and attentional selection
- Attention in schizophrenia
- The hyperfocusing hypothesis
- Attentional filtering
- Why schizophrenia should not be reduced to a simple "attention deficit"
- The relationship between attention, salience, meaning, and perception

The article ends by introducing the next conceptual step in the research journey:

> Attention asks: What should I prioritize?

> The next question is: What should I ignore?
