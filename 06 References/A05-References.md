# 1. Foundational Predictive Coding

## Rao & Ballard (1999)

Rao, R. P. N., & Ballard, D. H. (1999).

Predictive coding in the visual cortex: a functional interpretation of
some extra-classical receptive-field effects.

*Nature Neuroscience, 2*(1), 79–87.

https://doi.org/10.1038/4580

### Why it matters

A foundational computational model of predictive coding.

The paper proposes a hierarchical architecture in which predictions
are communicated downward while prediction-error information is
communicated upward.

### Supports

- hierarchical predictive coding;
- sensory prediction error;
- prediction/error distinction;
- computational rather than literal anatomical interpretation.

# 17. Predictive Processing — Current Debate

## 2026 Annual Review Perspective

Recent reviews emphasize that predictive processing remains an active
theoretical and empirical research area.

A central methodological issue is distinguishing observed neural
responses from the computational interpretation assigned to those
responses.

Therefore:

**Observed neural response ≠ automatically established prediction
error computation.**

This distinction is important throughout Article 05.

# 19. Evidence Classification

## Relatively Strong

- Organisms can learn from discrepancies between expected and observed
  outcomes.
- Reward-prediction-error models have strong experimental foundations.
- Dopaminergic neurons show reward-related responses consistent with
  prediction-error computations.
- Prediction-error concepts are central to reinforcement learning.

# 20. Important Scientific Caveats

The article should avoid the following claims:

"Prediction error causes schizophrenia."

"Dopamine is the prediction-error chemical."

"Hallucinations are prediction errors."

"Delusions are prediction errors."

"Psychosis is caused by excessive prediction error."

"Predictive processing has been proven to explain schizophrenia."

Instead use:

"Predictive-processing models provide a framework for investigating
how altered inference might contribute to some psychotic phenomena."

---

# 21. PIF Evidence Status

The Precision Instability Framework is a proposed research hypothesis.

It currently should be treated as:

**Hypothesis → operationalization → empirical testing**

rather than:

**Established theory → confirmed mechanism**

Future PIF research should test whether dynamic measures of precision
or precision-related proxies can explain variance in symptoms or
behavior beyond established models.
