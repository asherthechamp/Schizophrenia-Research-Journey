https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32506125/

# Core Scientific Caution

The article uses "filtering" as a conceptual umbrella for several related but distinguishable processes.

It does not assume that:

**P50 = attention**

or

**PPI = attention**

or

**filtering = inhibition**

or

**precision = filtering.**

These relationships are treated as interacting but non-identical levels of explanation.

---

# Key Takeaway

The brain does not appear to rely on a single filter that simply allows important information through and blocks everything else.

Instead, information processing involves interacting mechanisms of:

**selection → suppression → learning → prediction → inhibition → cognitive control**

The central research question therefore becomes increasingly dynamic:

> **Not simply: How strong is the filter?**

> **But: How flexible and stable is the brain's allocation of processing priority over time?**
