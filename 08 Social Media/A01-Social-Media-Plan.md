## Core Message

Schizophrenia is clinically recognizable, but its underlying biology is far more complex than a single-cause model suggests.

## Short Post

What exactly is schizophrenia?

The diagnosis is clinically useful and well established—but it does not map neatly onto one gene, one brain region, one neurotransmitter, or one biological mechanism.

Our 50-article research journey begins by asking a deeper question:

How does biology become experience?

## Key Distinction

Established science ≠ interpretation ≠ hypothesis.

The Precision Instability Framework is presented as a proposed hypothesis, not established science.
