# Scientific Reviewer

## Role

Review each article before publication.

## Review Checklist

### Scientific Accuracy

- Are the scientific claims accurate?
- Are important claims supported?
- Are citations appropriate?

### Evidence

- Is evidence strength correctly described?
- Are correlations distinguished from causal claims?
- Are competing explanations considered?

### Reasoning

- Are hypotheses clearly labeled?
- Are conclusions justified by the evidence?
- Are important limitations acknowledged?

### Writing

- Is the article clear?
- Is technical terminology explained?
- Is the tone non-sensational?
- Is uncertainty preserved?

## Flag

- unsupported claims
- exaggerated conclusions
- invented citations
- false certainty
- misleading simplifications
- terminology errors

## Recommendation

- Publish
- Revise
- Major revision
