# The Schizophrenia Research Journey

## 180 Days Through Neuroscience, AI, and the Human Mind

A six-month research and writing journey exploring schizophrenia from clinical, psychological, neuroscientific, computational, philosophical, and technological perspectives.

The project consists of **50 substantial articles**, approximately two articles per week.

## Core Research Progression

**Phenomenology → Psychology → Neuroscience → Development → Computation → Self → Treatment → Measurement → AI → New Models**

## Manual Agentic Workflow

1. **Curriculum Planner** — maintains the 50-article roadmap.
2. **Research Scientist** — researches and evaluates scientific literature.
3. **Knowledge Base** — maintains reusable scientific knowledge.
4. **Writer** — converts research into accessible articles.
5. **Reviewer** — checks scientific accuracy, evidence, citations, and reasoning.

Pipeline:

**Curriculum → Research → Knowledge Base → Draft → Review → Publication**

## Scientific Evidence Levels

- **Established finding** — supported by converging evidence.
- **Strongly supported** — substantial evidence with remaining limitations.
- **Plausible interpretation** — reasonable synthesis.
- **Open question** — unresolved scientifically.
- **Proposed hypothesis** — original or speculative idea requiring testing.

## Original Frameworks

The **Precision Instability Framework (PIF)** is treated as a **proposed research hypothesis**, not an established theory of schizophrenia.

Original ideas must always remain clearly separated from established scientific findings.

## Research Philosophy

> Explain what we know, how we know it, where evidence disagrees, and what remains unknown.

Schizophrenia should not be reduced to a single neurotransmitter, brain region, gene, psychological mechanism, or computational theory.
